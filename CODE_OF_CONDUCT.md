# Code of conduct

miracache adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as its code of conduct. Everyone who takes part in this project (in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and
any other project space) is expected to follow it.

## In short

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Welcome newcomers and people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- No harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing someone's
  private information without their permission.

This summary does not replace the full text of the Covenant, which applies in full.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it to the project maintainers at
[cache@mirastacklabs.ai](mailto:cache@mirastacklabs.ai). All reports will be reviewed and handled promptly and fairly,
and the privacy of the person reporting will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this
code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor, following the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant 2.1.
